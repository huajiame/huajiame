## 2. Lithium Battery & Powertrain Marine Transport Standards

All electric specialty vehicles exported by Huajia Machinery adhere to international maritime dangerous goods (IMDG) protocols:
- **UN 38.3 Testing Compliance**: Full lithium-ion (LiFePO4 / NMC) battery packs undergo altitude simulation, thermal cycling, vibration, shock, external short circuit, impact, overcharge, and forced discharge tests.
- **MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheet)** & Dangerous Goods Declarations issued per shipment.
- **Smart Battery Management System (BMS)**: Integrated with low-voltage disconnect, thermal runaway cutoff, and over-current protection.
- **Dual-Chemistry Support**: Standard high-reliability lead-acid gel batteries for cost-sensitive markets; high-density LiFePO4 batteries for rapid charging and extended operating lifespan.

## 3. 200-Point Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) Protocol

Before sealing into ocean containers, every Huajia vehicle undergoes a rigorous 200-point PDI audit:
1. **Chassis & Structural Frame**: Non-destructive weld testing, electrophoretic primer thickness test, salt-spray rust-resistance verification.
2. **Powertrain & Electrical**: Continuous load dyno testing (motor thermal rise, controller response), insulation resistance testing (> 50 MΩ), wire harness water-tightness.
3. **Braking & Safety Dynamics**: 100% full-stop hydraulic brake test on 20% incline, mechanical parking brake lock test, turning radius calibration.
4. **Cosmetic & Water Ingress**: Automotive-grade mirror finish gloss test, high-pressure rain chamber test (IPX6 waterproof seal verification for electrical components).
5. **Video & Photographic Evidence Log**: A complete digital PDI dossier (video walkaround + chassis number photos) is archived and shared with the buyer prior to final payment balance.

## 4. Left-Hand Drive (LHD) vs. Right-Hand Drive (RHD) Homologation

Huajia Machinery designs and manufactures vehicles compatible with regional traffic regulations worldwide:
- **LHD Standard**: Tailored for Mainland Europe, North America, Middle East (GCC), South America, and China.
- **RHD Customization**: Dedicated steering rack geometry, pedal placement, and dashboard layout for the UK, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Africa, Cyprus, Singapore, Malaysia, and the Caribbean.
- **Lighting & Signaling**: E-mark / DOT compliant headlamps, brake lights, amber turn signals, and side reflectors.

## 5. Commercial Packaging & Container Optimization

- **Knockdown (SKD / CKD)**: Reduces ocean freight volume by up to 50% for bulk dealer orders (e.g., 6-12 golf carts per 40HQ container).
- **Fully Assembled (CBU) in Reinforced Steel Crates**: Custom anti-vibration steel skids with waterproof shrink wrapping, enabling rapid roll-off and immediate deployment upon arrival.
- **Container Lashing & Chocking**: ISO-compliant heavy-duty polyester ratchet straps and hardwood wheel chocks bolted to container flooring.

## 6. Trade Terms & Verified Payment Methods

| Incoterm | Responsibility & Scope | Recommended For |
|---|---|---|
| **FOB (Free On Board)** | Seller delivers to China Port (Haikou/Shenzhen/Ningbo/Shanghai); Buyer manages ocean freight & insurance | Experienced importers with existing freight forwarder contracts |
