## 🏢 About Huajia Machinery

**Hainan Huajia Machinery Equipment Co., Ltd. (海南省铧甲机械设备有限公司)** is an export-oriented custom vehicle manufacturer headquartered in Hainan Free Trade Port, China. We specialize in the R&D, customization, and worldwide distribution of new energy specialty vehicles, commercial mobility platforms, and custom tourism transport.

### 🌟 Core Product Lineup

- **Electric Golf Carts & Buggies**: 2-seater, 4-seater, 6-seater, and white cargo golf carts for resorts, golf courses, and commercial campuses.
- **Vintage & Classic Sightseeing Cars**: 90s retro replica electric sports cars, open-top roadsters, and historic street shuttles.
- **Royal & Tourist Horse Carriages**: Handcrafted Victorian-style carriages, white crown royal coaches, and electric-assisted sightseeing carriages.
- **Mobile Food Trucks & Catering Trailers**: Customized pirate ship food trucks, concession trailers, and mobile vending units.
- **Utility Vehicles, ATVs & Mini Jeeps**: 150cc/200cc/350cc gas and electric mini jeeps, trailer jeeps, quad ATVs, and off-road UTVs.
- **Funeral Coaches & Hearses**: Custom 4-wheel and 6-wheel electric and horse-drawn royal funeral hearses for memorial ceremonies.
