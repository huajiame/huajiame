## 🏢 Company Profile

**Hainan Huajia Machinery Equipment Co., Ltd. (海南省铧甲机械设备有限公司)** is an export-driven vehicle engineering and manufacturing company headquartered in **Haikou, Hainan Free Trade Port (FTP), China**. 

We specialize in end-to-end design, custom powertrain integration, and worldwide delivery of specialty vehicles, commercial mobile units, and leisure transport platforms. Our vehicles are deployed worldwide across resorts, golf clubs, theme parks, municipal operations, commercial catering fleets, and private estates.
